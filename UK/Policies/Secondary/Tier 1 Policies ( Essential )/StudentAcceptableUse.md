# Student Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## Purpose

To ensure safe, responsible and effective use of technology by students.

## Scope

Applies to all pupils using:

- Trust devices
- BYOD devices where permitted
- School networks
- Cloud services

## Student Responsibilities

Students must:

- Use technology appropriately
- Protect passwords
- Report concerns immediately
- Respect others online

## Prohibited Activities

Students must not:

- Access inappropriate content
- Attempt to bypass filtering
- Cyberbully others
- Share personal information
- Download unauthorised applications

## Monitoring

Internet and device activity may be monitored to support safeguarding.
