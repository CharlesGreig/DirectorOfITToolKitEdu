# Multi-Academy Trust (MAT) IT Strategy 2026-2031

## A Strategic Framework for Digital Transformation, Cyber Security, Value for Money and Sustainability

This strategy is designed as a Trust-wide framework that aligns with the Department for Education (DfE) Digital and Technology Standards and wider statutory safeguarding requirements. It provides a 3-year delivery roadmap and a 5-year strategic vision while ensuring financial sustainability, operational resilience and measurable educational impact.

### Strategic Alignment

This strategy aligns particularly with:

- DfE Digital and Technology Standards including:
  - Broadband
  - Wireless Networks
  - Network Switching
  - Digital Leadership & Governance
  - Filtering & Monitoring
  - Cyber Security
- Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE)
- Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus
- UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018
- Schools Financial Value Standard (where applicable)
- Trust Strategic and School Improvement Plans

---

# 1. Strategic Vision

**To provide every pupil and member of staff with secure, reliable, sustainable and cost-effective technology that improves educational outcomes, increases efficiency, strengthens safeguarding and delivers exceptional value for public funding.**

## Strategic Principles

1. Educational impact first
2. Security by design
3. Value for money
4. Cloud-first where appropriate
5. Standardisation across the Trust
6. Sustainability and environmental responsibility
7. Data-driven decision making
8. Robust safeguarding and compliance

---

# 2. Strategic Objectives

## By 2029 (3-Year Objectives)

- All schools meet DfE Core Digital Standards.
- Full adoption of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Trust-wide technology standards implemented.
- Centralised asset and lifecycle management.
- Unified cyber security framework.
- Consistent filtering and monitoring solutions.
- Trust-wide reporting and analytics capability.
- Reduction in duplicated systems and licences.
- Green ICT reporting established.

## By 2031 (5-Year Objectives)

- Fully cloud-managed infrastructure where practical.
- High levels of automation for routine IT operations.
- Cyber Essentials Plus certification achieved Trust-wide.
- Strategic adoption of AI technologies.
- Digital inclusion embedded across the Trust.
- Reduction in ICT-related energy consumption.
- Technology recognised as a key enabler of educational excellence.

# 5. Infrastructure and Connectivity

Provide reliable, scalable and resilient infrastructure across all academies.

## 3-Year Priorities

### Network Infrastructure

- Gigabit-capable internet connectivity for all sites.
- Review and implement SD-WAN where appropriate.
- Core switching renewal programme.
- Enterprise-grade wireless coverage in all teaching, administrative and public spaces.

### Resilience

- Secondary internet connectivity at critical sites.
- Scheduled failover testing.
- Centralised infrastructure monitoring.

- Annual resilience testing completed.

## 5-Year Vision

- Automated configuration management.
- Full visibility across all Trust infrastructure.

# Strategic Outcome

By 2031, the Trust will operate a secure, standardised, cloud-first, financially sustainable and environmentally responsible technology estate that:

- Fully aligns with DfE Digital and Technology Standards.
- Supports statutory safeguarding obligations.
- Delivers measurable value for money.
- Reduces waste and environmental impact.
- Enables innovation and digital transformation.
- Supports exceptional educational outcomes.
- Provides resilient and secure technology services across every academy.

Technology will be recognised not simply as an operational requirement, but as a strategic enabler of educational excellence, operational efficiency and long-term sustainability.
