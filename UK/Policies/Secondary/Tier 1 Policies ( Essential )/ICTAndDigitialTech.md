# ICT & Digital Technology Policy

## Purpose

To establish the strategic framework governing the acquisition, management, use and security of digital technologies across the Trust.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Agency and contract staff
- Governors and Trustees
- Volunteers
- Third-party suppliers with access to Trust systems
- Pupils using Trust technology

## Objectives

- Support teaching and learning through effective use of technology
- Ensure value for money from ICT investment
- Reduce risk through standardisation
- Support safeguarding and compliance
- Promote secure and sustainable technology practices

## Policy Statements

- ICT investments shall align with Trust strategic priorities.
- Technology standards shall be maintained and reviewed annually.
- All technology purchases shall follow Trust procurement procedures.
- Schools shall adopt approved Trust platforms wherever possible.
- ICT risks shall be recorded and managed through the Trust risk framework.
- ICT services shall be monitored against agreed performance targets.
