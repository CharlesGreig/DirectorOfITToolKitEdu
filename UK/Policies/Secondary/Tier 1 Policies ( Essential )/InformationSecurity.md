# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

To protect Trust information, systems and services from unauthorised access, alteration, loss or disclosure.

## Scope

Applies to all information assets.

## Objectives

- Preserve confidentiality
- Maintain integrity
- Ensure availability

## Requirements

- MFA must be enabled where supported.
- Access shall be granted on a least-privilege basis.
- Sensitive information must be encrypted when appropriate.
- Security incidents must be reported immediately.
- Users shall follow password requirements.

## Governance

Information security shall be reviewed annually as part of the Trust risk management process.
