# Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy

## Purpose

To govern the responsible use of artificial intelligence technologies.

## Objectives

- Promote innovation
- Protect personal data
- Support safeguarding
- Maintain educational integrity

## Approved Use

AI may be used for:

- Administrative support
- Research assistance
- Content drafting
- Workflow automation

## Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- Submit confidential information without approval
- Use unauthorised AI services
- Rely solely on AI-generated outputs for critical decisions

## User Responsibilities

Users must verify AI-generated content before use.

## Governance

AI solutions shall undergo risk assessment prior to adoption.
