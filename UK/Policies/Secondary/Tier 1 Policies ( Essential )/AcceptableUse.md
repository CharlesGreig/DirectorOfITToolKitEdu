# Staff Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## Purpose

To define the acceptable use of Trust technology resources by staff.

## Scope

Applies to all staff using:

- Computers
- Mobile devices
- Cloud services
- Internet access
- Email systems
- Collaboration platforms

## Acceptable Use

Staff may:

- Access systems required for their role
- Use approved software and services
- Access professional resources

## Prohibited Activities

Staff must not:

- Share passwords
- Circumvent security controls
- Install unauthorised software
- Access inappropriate material
- Share confidential information without authorisation
- Use Trust systems for unlawful activities

## Monitoring

The Trust reserves the right to monitor:

- Internet activity
- Email activity
- Device usage
- Security events

## Non-Compliance

Failure to comply may result in:

- Withdrawal of access
- Disciplinary action
- Legal action where appropriate
