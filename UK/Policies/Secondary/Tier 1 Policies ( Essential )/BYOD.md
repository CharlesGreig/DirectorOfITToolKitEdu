# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy

## Purpose

To regulate the use of personally owned devices accessing Trust systems.

## Scope

Applies to staff and authorised users connecting personal devices.

## Requirements

- Devices must be secured with a passcode.
- Devices must be regularly updated.
- Trust data must not be stored locally unless authorised.
- MFA must be used where required.

## Support

The Trust does not provide support for personal devices beyond connectivity assistance.

## Removal of Access

The Trust may revoke access where security requirements are not met.
