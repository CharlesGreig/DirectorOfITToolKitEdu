## IT Risk Register

This is a list of Risks that you should consider and have a responsibility / mitigation plan for

### Power Outage/

What happens : Power Fails

IT Response : If the power fails, UPS should allow for graceful shutdown of systems to prevent damage to equipment - equipment should not be powered back on until stable power's return is confirmed by Site Manager.

Responsibility : Site Team / Facilities

Impact : Systems down until power restored.
