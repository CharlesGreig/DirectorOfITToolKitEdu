# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

## Purpose

To ensure continued delivery of critical services during disruption.

## Objectives

- Maintain critical operations
- Reduce downtime
- Improve organisational resilience

## Scenarios

The Trust shall plan for:

- Network outages
- Internet failure
- Power outages
- Cyber attacks
- Cloud service disruption
- Building loss

## Testing

Business continuity arrangements shall be tested annually.

## Review

This policy shall be reviewed annually and after any major incident.
