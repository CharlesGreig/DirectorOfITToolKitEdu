# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

To establish procedures for responding to ICT and cyber security incidents.

## Objectives

- Restore operations quickly
- Minimise impact
- Protect information assets
- Meet legal obligations

## Incident Categories

- Security incidents
- Data breaches
- Service outages
- Malware infections
- Ransomware events

## Process

1. Detection
2. Reporting
3. Classification
4. Containment
5. Recovery
6. Review

## Escalation

Significant incidents shall be escalated to senior leadership immediately.
