# Backup and Recovery Policy

## Purpose

To ensure critical information can be recovered following a disruption.

## Objectives

- Protect business-critical information
- Support disaster recovery
- Minimise downtime

## Requirements

- Critical systems must be backed up.
- Backup schedules shall be documented.
- Recovery testing shall occur annually.
- Backup integrity shall be monitored.

## Ownership

System owners are responsible for ensuring successful backup coverage.
